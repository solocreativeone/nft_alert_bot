# reportlab 4.x AND 5.x are pure-Python wheels with NO bundled _renderPM C

# "cannot import desired renderPM backend rlPyCairo" on a clean install.

# X11 dev headers to compile. cairosvg is the pure-wheel alternative and is what
# notifier._rasterize_svg tries first.
